Research

Cellular state, communication and function.

My research examines how cellular stress, ageing and disease alter molecular communication between cells and how these changes influence cellular and tissue function. I approach these questions through mammalian cell biology, extracellular-vesicle research, quantitative functional assays, molecular analysis, microscopy and proteomics.

Research Question

How do changes in cellular state alter molecular communication between cells, and how do these changes influence ageing, tissue function and disease?

My scientific trajectory has developed from redox biology and cellular stress through endothelial ageing and senescence, extracellular-vesicle biology, quantitative molecular profiling and functional cargo-delivery research.

These areas are connected by a common interest: understanding how altered cellular states are communicated to neighbouring cells and how those signals influence biological function.

I

Cellular State

Cellular Ageing, Senescence & Endothelial Biology

How does endothelial ageing change cellular phenotype and communication with neighbouring cells?

My doctoral research within the French-German Marie Skłodowska-Curie TiCardio programme investigated how ageing and cellular senescence alter endothelial-cell biology and intercellular communication.

I worked with primary human endothelial-cell models and replicative-senescence systems, combining prolonged cell culture, cellular phenotyping, stress and senescence assessment, endothelial activation studies and recipient-cell experiments.

Experimental systems

Primary human endothelial cells and prolonged cell-culture models.

Cellular phenotyping

Senescence, cellular-stress, cell-cycle and endothelial activation readouts.

Functional biology

Recipient-cell experiments and functional biological assessment.

II

Intercellular Communication

Extracellular Vesicles & Intercellular Communication

How does cellular state influence communication between cells through extracellular vesicles?

Extracellular vesicles became a central connection between my work on cellular ageing and my broader interest in intercellular communication.

During my doctoral research, I investigated ageing-associated changes in endothelial extracellular vesicles and evaluated their functional effects using recipient-cell experimental systems.

EV preparation

Extracellular-vesicle isolation and preparation workflows.

Characterisation

Particle analysis, biophysical characterisation and cytometric approaches.

Functional communication

EV tracking and recipient-cell functional experiments.

III

Functional Delivery

Functional Cargo Delivery

How can intracellular delivery be evaluated through quantitative functional readouts?

During my fixed-term postdoctoral appointment in the Lavieu Lab at Université Paris Cité, I worked with quantitative mammalian cell-based systems for evaluating functional cargo delivery.

My contribution focused on optimisation and standardisation of quantitative functional assays, experimental controls and reproducible workflows. I did not originate the underlying delivery technology.

Postdoctoral Research

Lavieu Lab · Université Paris Cité · Paris, France

Optimised and standardised quantitative NanoLuc-based functional assays in engineered HeLa donor–recipient systems to evaluate EV-mediated cargo delivery. The work included reproducible assay workflows and collaborative evaluation of modified extracellular vesicles.

Quantitative cell-based assays

My work focused on establishing robust and reproducible mammalian cell-based workflows for quantitative assessment of functional intracellular delivery.

  • Mammalian cell-based assay optimisation
  • Reporter-based functional readouts
  • Transfection optimisation
  • Experimental controls
  • Quantitative analysis
  • Reproducibility and structured troubleshooting

Collaborative delivery research

I also contributed to collaborative studies evaluating cellular uptake and functional intracellular delivery using modified EV delivery systems.

A central focus of this work was quantitative assessment of whether intracellular delivery produced a measurable functional readout.
IV

Molecular Discovery

Proteomics & Molecular Discovery

How can quantitative molecular profiling support biological investigation of ageing and extracellular-vesicle biology?

Proteomics represents the molecular-discovery component of my ageing and extracellular-vesicle research.

During my doctoral work and research secondment at ISAS Dortmund, I integrated quantitative proteomics with experimental studies of endothelial extracellular vesicles.

This work combined experimental sample preparation, quantitative proteomic datasets, downstream analysis, pathway interpretation and biological prioritisation.

Experimental

S-Trap sample preparation and preparation of extracellular-vesicle samples for LC-MS/MS proteomics.

Quantitative analysis

MaxQuant, Perseus, R and exploratory Bioconductor workflows.

Biological interpretation

Cytoscape, Reactome, FunRich, pathway-level interpretation and biological prioritisation.
