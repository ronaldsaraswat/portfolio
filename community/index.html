Gene & Tonic Cité

Learn. Discuss. Meet. Connect. Explore.

Gene & Tonic Cité is a resident-led Life Sciences and Medicine community based at the Cité internationale universitaire de Paris.

I co-founded the initiative with Sofia Tagkalidou to create a more informal and interdisciplinary alternative to the conventional scientific seminar.

Gene & Tonic brings together researchers, doctoral and Master's students, healthcare professionals and people interested in science through accessible presentations, discussion, debate, networking and shared scientific experiences.

The aim is not to simplify science until its substance disappears, but to make specialised ideas easier to enter, discuss and connect across disciplines and cultures.

Five Chapters

Science from cells to space and nature.

The programme deliberately crosses scientific disciplines rather than remaining within a single biomedical theme.

Chapter 01 · March 2026

Immune Tolerance

Gene & Tonic launched at Maison du Mexique with accessible discussion of regulatory T cells, FOXP3 and immune tolerance, alongside participant introductions designed to turn an audience into a scientific community.

Chapter 02 · March 2026

Brain Organoids & Biological Rhythms

Dr Miguel Olmedo introduced brain organoids and biological rhythms, followed by an interdisciplinary discussion around artificial intelligence in the life sciences.

Chapter 03

Human Biology Beyond Earth

An Astro/Bio crossover explored Artemis, human physiology in space and the biological challenges of sustaining human health beyond Earth's protective environment.

Chapter 04

Ideas Shaped at the Cité

Chapter 05 · July 2026

The Format

More than a seminar series.

Gene & Tonic is designed around participation rather than passive scientific listening.

Learn Accessible scientific presentations grounded in real research
Discuss Questions, debates, polls and interdisciplinary conversation
Meet Introductions and informal networking across scientific backgrounds
Connect Community-building across institutions, countries and disciplines
Explore Science-oriented cultural outings beyond conventional seminar spaces

Why It Matters

Scientific communities are built between people.

Research does not progress through experiments alone.

It also depends on people being able to explain ideas, challenge interpretations, cross disciplinary boundaries and encounter perspectives they would not necessarily meet inside their own laboratory.

Gene & Tonic grew from that idea: that international scientific communities become stronger when conversation, curiosity and shared experience create connections between people.
