Science, Leadership & Community

Science grows through conversation.

Alongside laboratory research, I value scientific communication, interdisciplinary exchange, mentoring and community building. I have contributed to research networks, scientific representation and public-facing initiatives designed to make scientific ideas more accessible and collaborative.

Paris · 2026–Present

Gene & Tonic

Co-founder & Organiser

Building informal spaces for scientific conversation and community.

I co-founded and organise Gene & Tonic at the Cité internationale universitaire de Paris, an interdisciplinary science-communication and networking initiative bringing together researchers, students and members of the life-science community.

The programme combines accessible scientific talks, discussion, interdisciplinary exchange and social interaction to create an environment in which people can engage with science beyond formal laboratory and conference settings.

Gene & Tonic received €1,500 in FIR 2026 project funding from the Cité internationale universitaire de Paris to support its programme and development.

Mentoring & Research Support

Scientific training also involves helping others build reliable experimental thinking.

During research projects, I have provided experimental guidance and scientific support to MSc and PhD researchers, including discussion of experimental design, troubleshooting, quantitative interpretation and reproducible laboratory workflows.

I value mentoring as a collaborative process: helping researchers understand not only how an experiment is performed, but why particular controls, comparisons and analytical decisions matter.
