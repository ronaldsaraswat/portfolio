Gene & Tonic Cité

Life Sciences & Medicine at CIUP.

Gene & Tonic Cité is a resident-led Life Sciences and Medicine community at the Cité internationale universitaire de Paris.

I co-founded the initiative with Sofia Tagkalidou to create an informal interdisciplinary format combining accessible scientific presentations, discussion, networking and community activities.

Participants include researchers, doctoral and Master's students, healthcare professionals and people interested in science.

Five Chapters

Programme themes.

The first five chapters crossed immunology, organoid biology, space biology, scientific history and natural science.

Chapter 01 · March 2026Immune Tolerance

Regulatory T cells, FOXP3 and immune tolerance, combined with participant introductions.

Chapter 02 · March 2026Brain Organoids & Biological Rhythms

Guest presentation by Dr Miguel Olmedo, followed by discussion of artificial intelligence in the life sciences.

Chapter 03Human Biology Beyond Earth

Artemis, human physiology in space and the biological challenges of sustaining human health beyond Earth.

Chapter 04Ideas Shaped at the Cité

A short exploration of CIUP's scientific and intellectual history.

Chapter 05 · July 2026Exploring the Natural World

A community scientific outing to the Grande Galerie de l'Évolution, Jardin des Plantes and Grandes Serres, followed by a shared picnic. More than 20 participants registered; participation was capped at 30.

Beyond Gene & Tonic

Outreach and mentoring.

Science communication has also been part of my research experience outside Paris.

During Mainzer Science Week in 2022, I contributed to “A Sip of Science”, helping communicate the scientific activities of the TiCardio consortium in an informal public-engagement setting.

I have also supported Master's and doctoral researchers through experimental reasoning, troubleshooting and scientific discussion.
