02Review Article2021

Atherosclerosis

Multifaceted role of extracellular vesicles in atherosclerosis

Konkoth A, Saraswat R, Dubrou C, Sabatier F, Leroyer AS, Lacroix R, Duchez AC, Dignat-George F.

Atherosclerosis. 2021;319:121–131.

Paper in Brief

A review of extracellular vesicles in atherosclerosis, including their relationships with inflammation, coagulation, vascular dysfunction, angiogenesis and cellular senescence.

The article also discusses methodological standardisation, biomarker potential and therapeutic applications of extracellular vesicles in cardiovascular disease.

Scientific ThemeExtracellular vesicles · Atherosclerosis · Intercellular communication
Translational ContextBiomarkers · Standardisation · Therapeutic potential

03Methods Chapter2019

Methods in Molecular Biology

Diagonal Electrophoresis for the Detection of Proteins Involved in Disulfide Bonds

Saraswat R, McDonagh B.

In: Kurien BT, Scofield RH, editors. Electrophoretic Separation of Proteins: Methods and Protocols. Methods in Molecular Biology. 2019;1855:279–286.

Chapter in Brief

A methods chapter describing diagonal electrophoresis for investigating proteins involved in disulfide-bond formation and redox-sensitive protein regulation.

Sequential non-reducing and reducing electrophoresis distinguishes proteins according to disulfide-bonding behaviour and can be combined with downstream protein identification.

Scientific ThemeRedox proteomics · Protein disulfides · Redox signalling
