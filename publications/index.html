Review Article · 2021

Extracellular vesicles in atherosclerosis.

Atherosclerosis

Multifaceted role of extracellular vesicles in atherosclerosis

Konkoth A, Saraswat R, Dubrou C, Sabatier F, Leroyer AS, Lacroix R, Duchez AC, Dignat-George F.

Atherosclerosis. 2021;319:121–131.

Paper in Brief

This review examines extracellular vesicles as biologically active messengers throughout the development and progression of atherosclerosis. It connects EV-mediated communication with inflammation, coagulation, vascular dysfunction, angiogenesis and cellular senescence.

The article also considers the dual and context-dependent effects of extracellular vesicles, methodological and standardisation challenges within the field, and their potential development as cardiovascular biomarkers and therapeutic tools.

Scientific Theme Extracellular vesicles · Atherosclerosis · Intercellular communication
Translational Context Biomarkers · Standardisation · Therapeutic potential

Position Paper · 2023

Blood coagulation & beyond.

Thrombosis and Haemostasis

Blood Coagulation and Beyond: Position Paper from the Fourth Maastricht Consensus Conference on Thrombosis

Akbulut AC et al., including Ronald Saraswat.

Thrombosis and Haemostasis. 2023;123(8):808–839.

Paper in Brief

This collaborative position paper synthesises discussions from the Fourth Maastricht Consensus Conference on Thrombosis across fundamental, translational and clinical perspectives.

The conference addressed the broader biological roles of coagulation, emerging mechanisms of thrombosis, strategies for reducing bleeding risk, haemostasis in extracorporeal systems and major clinical dilemmas in antithrombotic management.

My Contribution

As a TiCardio doctoral researcher, I contributed to the conference discussions and subsequent manuscript development around microbiome–thrombosis interactions and extracellular-vesicle biology, working with participating principal investigators to review and revise this part of the collaborative position paper.

Format International consensus conference and collaborative position paper

Methods Chapter · 2019

Detecting protein disulfide interactions.

Methods in Molecular Biology

Diagonal Electrophoresis for the Detection of Proteins Involved in Disulfide Bonds

Saraswat R, McDonagh B.

In: Kurien BT, Scofield RH, editors. Electrophoretic Separation of Proteins: Methods and Protocols. Methods in Molecular Biology. 2019;1855:279–286.

Chapter in Brief

This methods chapter describes diagonal electrophoresis as an approach for investigating proteins involved in disulfide-bond formation and redox-sensitive protein regulation.

Sequential electrophoresis under non-reducing and reducing conditions allows proteins with different disulfide-bonding behaviours to be distinguished according to their migration relative to the gel diagonal.

The approach can be combined with downstream protein identification methods to investigate proteins involved in redox signalling pathways.

Scientific Theme Redox proteomics · Protein disulfides · Redox signalling
Publication Type Experimental methods chapter
