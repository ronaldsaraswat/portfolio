02 Review Article 2021

Atherosclerosis

Multifaceted role of extracellular vesicles in atherosclerosis

Konkoth A, Saraswat R, Dubrou C, Sabatier F, Leroyer AS, Lacroix R, Duchez AC, Dignat-George F.

Atherosclerosis. 2021;319:121–131.

Paper in Brief

This review examines extracellular vesicles as biologically active messengers throughout the development and progression of atherosclerosis, connecting EV-mediated communication with inflammation, coagulation, vascular dysfunction, angiogenesis and cellular senescence.

The article also considers the context-dependent effects of extracellular vesicles, methodological and standardisation challenges within the field, and their potential development as cardiovascular biomarkers and therapeutic tools.

Scientific Theme Extracellular vesicles · Atherosclerosis · Intercellular communication
Translational Context Biomarkers · Standardisation · Therapeutic potential

03 Methods Chapter 2019

Methods in Molecular Biology

Diagonal Electrophoresis for the Detection of Proteins Involved in Disulfide Bonds

Saraswat R, McDonagh B.

In: Kurien BT, Scofield RH, editors. Electrophoretic Separation of Proteins: Methods and Protocols. Methods in Molecular Biology. 2019;1855:279–286.

Chapter in Brief

This methods chapter describes diagonal electrophoresis as an approach for investigating proteins involved in disulfide-bond formation and redox-sensitive protein regulation.

Sequential electrophoresis under non-reducing and reducing conditions allows proteins with different disulfide-bonding behaviours to be distinguished according to their migration relative to the gel diagonal. The approach can be combined with downstream protein identification methods to investigate proteins involved in redox signalling pathways.

Scientific Theme Redox proteomics · Protein disulfides · Redox signalling
Publication Type Experimental methods chapter
