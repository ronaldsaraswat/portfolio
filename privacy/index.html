Privacy

Privacy notice.

How personal data are handled when you use this scientific portfolio or send a professional enquiry through the contact form.

This website is a personal scientific portfolio operated by Ronald Saraswat. Personal data are collected only where necessary to operate the site, respond to enquiries and provide the requested portfolio content.

Controller: Ronald SaraswatContact: Website contact formLast updated: 12 August 2026

Contact-form data

When you use the contact form, the following information may be collected:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • organisation or institution, if you choose to provide it;
  • subject and message content; and
  • technical information required to submit and protect the form from abuse.

Name, email address, subject and message are required to submit the form. Organisation or institution is optional.

Please do not submit special-category or highly sensitive personal data through the contact form unless it is genuinely necessary for your enquiry.

Purpose and legal basis

Contact-form data are used only to receive, assess and respond to scientific, professional, collaboration, recruitment, outreach or related enquiries.

The legal basis is the legitimate interest in receiving and responding to professional communications and maintaining necessary correspondence relating to those enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Who receives the data

Contact-form submissions are processed by Formspree, which provides the form-processing service used by this website. Submissions may be stored in the Formspree inbox and forwarded by email so that I can receive and respond to them.

Data may therefore be accessible to me, Formspree as form-processing provider, and the email service provider used to receive or respond to the message, only to the extent necessary for those purposes.

Retention

Contact enquiries and related correspondence are normally retained for up to 12 months after the last meaningful correspondence. They may be retained for longer where reasonably necessary for an ongoing professional relationship, recruitment process, collaboration, legal claim or legal obligation.

Hosting and technical data

This portfolio is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes.

I do not currently run my own advertising trackers or visitor-analytics service on this portfolio.

External links and media previews

The portfolio contains links to external scientific, institutional and social platforms. The External Mentions page also displays a limited number of media thumbnails supplied from external source servers. When your browser requests externally hosted content, the relevant provider may receive ordinary technical request information such as your IP address, browser information and request time.

Once you follow an external link, the destination site's own privacy terms apply.

International processing

Some technical service providers used by the site are based outside the European Economic Area. Formspree states that it relies on Standard Contractual Clauses as a data processor for GDPR purposes. Other providers apply their own international-transfer safeguards as described in their privacy documentation.

Your data-protection rights

Subject to the conditions of applicable data-protection law, you may request access to personal data concerning you, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, restriction of processing, or object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise these rights or ask a privacy question, use the website contact form. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the French data-protection authority, the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL).

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated if the site's data-processing practices or service providers change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
