About

My research sits at the intersection of extracellular-vesicle biology, cellular ageing and quantitative cell biology.

Across academic and translational research environments, I have investigated how cells communicate, how ageing alters cellular and extracellular signals, and how quantitative biological systems can be used to measure functional intercellular cargo delivery.

My work combines mammalian cell models, extracellular-vesicle biology, functional assays, proteomics and quantitative data analysis to address biologically and translationally relevant questions.
