About

Biology, communication and function.

I am a cell and molecular biologist studying how cellular stress, ageing and disease alter intercellular communication and biological function.

My scientific journey has progressed from redox and cellular-stress biology through endothelial ageing and senescence, extracellular-vesicle research, quantitative proteomics and functional mammalian cell-based assays.

I am particularly interested in connecting mechanistic biology with quantitative experimentation and translational R&D, including future technologies relevant to therapeutics, diagnostics, precision medicine and personalised healthcare.

Current Status

Based in Paris, France. Open to new research, scientific collaboration and translational R&D opportunities.
