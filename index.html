About

Research across cells, signals and function.

I am a cell and molecular biologist whose research career has developed across Ireland, France and Germany.

My work has included redox biology and oxidative stress, endothelial ageing and senescence, extracellular-vesicle biology, quantitative proteomics and mammalian cell-based functional assays.

Current Status

Based in Paris, France. Open to research scientist, postdoctoral and translational biomedical R&D opportunities.

Research

Four areas of completed research.

Each area reflects a defined part of my experimental training and research work.

Endothelial Ageing & Senescence

Primary human endothelial-cell models used to study cellular ageing, senescence and downstream biological effects.

Extracellular Vesicles

EV preparation, characterisation and recipient-cell experiments focused on intercellular communication.

Functional Cell Biology

Quantitative reporter-based mammalian cell assays for evaluating functional intracellular delivery.

Proteomics

Quantitative molecular profiling and pathway-level interpretation of extracellular-vesicle protein data.

Science & Community

Gene & Tonic Cité and scientific outreach.

I co-founded Gene & Tonic Cité, a resident-led Life Sciences and Medicine community at the Cité internationale universitaire de Paris.

The programme combines accessible scientific presentations, discussion, networking and science-oriented community activities. I also develop AI-assisted conceptual scientific visuals for education and communication.
