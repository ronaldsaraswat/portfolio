Scientific Profile

Cellular biology, communication and quantitative function.

I am a cell and molecular biologist whose research has developed across Ireland, France and Germany.

My work connects cellular stress and ageing, extracellular-vesicle biology, quantitative molecular profiling and functional mammalian cell-based assays. I am particularly interested in experimentally rigorous approaches that make biological mechanisms measurable and interpretable.

Current Status

Based in Paris, France. Open to research scientist, postdoctoral, scientific collaboration and translational biomedical R&D opportunities.

Research

Four connected areas of experimental work.

A programme linking altered cellular states, intercellular communication, functional readouts and molecular discovery.

Endothelial Ageing & Senescence

Primary human endothelial-cell models used to investigate cellular ageing, senescence and downstream biological effects.

Extracellular Vesicles

EV preparation, characterisation and recipient-cell experiments focused on intercellular communication.

Functional Cell Biology

Quantitative reporter-based mammalian cell assays for evaluating functional intracellular delivery.

Proteomics & Molecular Discovery

Quantitative molecular profiling, pathway-level interpretation and biological prioritisation of extracellular-vesicle protein data.

Data-driven Scientific software Controlled public beta

VennScope

I independently developed VennScope for transparent comparison of gene, protein and other identifier sets. It supports Venn and UpSet visualisation, intersection inspection, explicit identifier mapping, overlap statistics and publication-oriented export.

Omics-derived intersections can be exported for downstream Gene Ontology or pathway-enrichment workflows in dedicated analysis tools.
