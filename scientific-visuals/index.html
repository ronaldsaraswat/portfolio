The Idea

Scientific concepts through visual language.

Biological concepts are often easier to understand when molecular and cellular relationships can be seen rather than described only in text.

Open Scientific Visuals is a developing initiative exploring how AI-assisted illustration can support accurate, accessible and engaging scientific communication.

The aim is not to replace experimental figures, scientific diagrams derived from data or conventional scientific illustration. Instead, the collection is intended for conceptual explanation: helping audiences understand processes, relationships and biological ideas.

Important Distinction

Conceptual art is not experimental data.

Illustrations in this collection are conceptual scientific communication materials.

They should not be interpreted as microscopy, quantitative datasets, experimental figures, computational reconstructions or direct representations of experimental observations.

Where AI-assisted generation is used, that assistance is disclosed. Scientific concepts, terminology and interpretation are reviewed before a visual is considered for release within the open-use collection.

Reuse & Attribution

Open only when explicitly stated.

Appearance on this website does not automatically make an image open for reuse.

Only visuals carrying an explicit open-use notice and stated reuse terms should be treated as part of the reusable collection.

Portfolio photographs, website graphics, research figures and scientific illustrations without an explicit open-use notice remain outside the open collection.

A formal licensing model will be assigned before the first visual is released for unrestricted public reuse.

Attribution

Clear scientific provenance.

Where a visual is formally released for reuse, its page will provide a recommended attribution statement.

A possible attribution format for future released visuals is:

Example

AI-assisted scientific illustration · Concept and scientific review: Ronald Saraswat, PhD

The exact wording and licensing terms will be finalised before the first open-use release.
