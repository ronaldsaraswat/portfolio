Open Scientific Visuals.

A growing collection of AI-assisted scientific illustrations developed for education, presentations, scientific communication and community use.

The Project

Making science easier to see.

Scientific ideas are often easier to understand when complex biological processes can be communicated visually.

I am developing Open Scientific Visuals as a growing collection of conceptual scientific illustrations connected with cell biology, ageing, extracellular vesicles and related areas of biomedical research.

The aim is to create visually engaging resources that can support teaching, scientific presentations, student projects and public science communication.

Artificial intelligence may be used as part of the illustration process, while scientific concepts, terminology and biological interpretation are reviewed before a visual is released into the open-use collection.

Collection Principles

Useful. Transparent. Scientific.

The collection is designed as a science- communication resource rather than a repository of experimental data.

Scientifically Reviewed

Visuals entering the open collection will be reviewed for conceptual scientific accuracy before public release.

AI-Assisted & Transparent

Where generative AI contributes to the creation of an illustration, this will be stated clearly rather than presenting the work as conventionally drawn scientific art.

Conceptual, Not Experimental Data

Illustrations are educational representations of biological concepts. They should not be interpreted as experimental figures, microscopy data or quantitative results.

Clear Reuse Terms

Every visual released for open use will include its own reuse guidance and recommended attribution.

Every Visual

Context before download.

Open Scientific Visuals will provide more than an image file.

Each released illustration will include enough scientific and practical context for users to understand what they are looking at and how the visual can be used.

Scientific Title Clear description of the biological concept represented
Scientific Context Concise explanation of the mechanisms or concepts illustrated
Suggested Uses Teaching, presentations, scientific communication and educational projects
Reuse Guidance Explicit reuse status and recommended attribution
Transparency AI-assistance and conceptual nature clearly identified
Related Science Links to relevant research topics and scientific context

Reuse

Open when explicitly stated.

Only visuals carrying an explicit open-use notice on this website should be treated as part of the freely reusable collection.

Other photographs, research graphics, portfolio images and illustrations appearing elsewhere on this website are not automatically released for reuse simply because they appear on the Scientific Visuals page or elsewhere in the portfolio.

Each future open visual will display its applicable reuse terms and recommended attribution directly alongside the image.

Science Communication

A small contribution to open science.

Good scientific visuals can make complex biological ideas more accessible.

My aim is to contribute useful visual resources that researchers, students, educators and science communicators can draw upon without requiring specialist illustration resources for every concept.

The project also complements my broader interest in scientific communication, interdisciplinary exchange and community initiatives.

Suggestions

What should we illustrate?

The collection will grow progressively as new scientific concepts are developed into visual resources.

Researchers, educators and science communicators are welcome to suggest biological concepts that could benefit from a clear conceptual illustration.

Suggestions do not imply that every requested topic will be added, but they can help identify areas where openly reusable scientific visuals would be particularly useful.
