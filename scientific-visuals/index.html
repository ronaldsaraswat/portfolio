VennScope

Browser-based scientific set analysis for gene, protein and other identifier lists, including Venn and UpSet views, intersection inspection, explicit identifier mapping, overlap statistics and publication-oriented figure export.

For omics workflows, selected intersections can be exported for downstream Gene Ontology or pathway-enrichment analysis using dedicated functional-interpretation tools.

Reuse Status

Purpose

Explain concepts, not experimental results.

The visuals are intended to make cellular and molecular relationships easier to discuss without presenting conceptual art as data.

They are not substitutes for microscopy, quantitative datasets, experimental figures or data-derived scientific diagrams.

Standards

How a visual is prepared for use.

Scientific clarity, disclosure and reuse conditions are handled explicitly.

Scientific Review

Biological concepts, terminology and interpretation are checked before a visual is presented for scientific communication.

AI Assistance Disclosed

AI-assisted generation is stated rather than presented as conventionally drawn scientific artwork.

Conceptual Scope

Each image is built around a defined biological idea rather than decorative complexity.

Explicit Licensing

Only images carrying a stated licence are released for reuse. Other site images remain outside the reusable collection.

Reuse & Attribution
